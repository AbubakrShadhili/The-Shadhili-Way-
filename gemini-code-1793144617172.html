About The Tariqa Shadhiliyya

Welcome to The Shadhili Way—a dedicated resource exploring the rich tradition and practices of the Tariqa Shadhiliyya, founded by Imam Abul Hasan al-Shadhili. Our mission is to make the classical teachings of remembrance (dhikr), gratitude (shukr), and spiritual illumination clear and approachable for seekers everywhere.
